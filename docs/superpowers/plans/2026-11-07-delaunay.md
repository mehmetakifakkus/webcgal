### Görev 4: Sözde kod, presetler, adım üreteci

**Files:**
- Create: `pseudocode.ts`, `presets.ts`, `delaunaySteps.ts`
- Test: `delaunaySteps.test.ts`, `presets.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- `DELAUNAY_PSEUDOCODE: string[]` — tam olarak şu 21 satır (başlıklar `PSEUDOCODE_HEADINGS = [0, 15]`; girinti 4'er boşluk):
  ```
  0  DelaunayTriangulation(P)
  1      Let p_0 be the lexicographically highest point of P (the rightmost among the points with the largest y)
  2      Let p_−1 and p_−2 be two symbolic points far away, so that P lies inside the triangle p_0 p_−1 p_−2
  3      Initialize T as the triangulation consisting of the single triangle p_0 p_−1 p_−2
  4      Compute a random permutation p_1, p_2, …, p_n of P \ {p_0}
  5      for r ← 1 to n
  6          Find the triangle p_i p_j p_k ∈ T that contains p_r
  7          if p_r lies in the interior of the triangle p_i p_j p_k
  8              Add edges from p_r to p_i, p_j and p_k, splitting the triangle into three
  9              LegalizeEdge(p_r, p_i p_j, T); LegalizeEdge(p_r, p_j p_k, T); LegalizeEdge(p_r, p_k p_i, T)
  10         else (p_r lies on an edge, say p_i p_j, shared with the triangle p_i p_j p_l)
  11             Add edges from p_r to p_k and to p_l, splitting the two triangles into four
  12             LegalizeEdge(p_r, p_i p_l, T); LegalizeEdge(p_r, p_l p_j, T); LegalizeEdge(p_r, p_j p_k, T); LegalizeEdge(p_r, p_k p_i, T)
  13     Discard p_−1 and p_−2 with all their incident edges from T
  14     return T
  15 LegalizeEdge(p_r, p_i p_j, T)
  16     if p_i p_j is illegal
  17         Let p_i p_j p_k be the triangle adjacent to p_r p_i p_j along p_i p_j
  18         Replace p_i p_j with p_r p_k (flip the edge)
  19         LegalizeEdge(p_r, p_i p_k, T)
  20         LegalizeEdge(p_r, p_k p_j, T)
  ```
- `DELAUNAY_PRESETS: { name; points: Pt[]; order: number[]; expected: { triangles: number; flips: number } }[]` — koordinatlar ve `order` tam olarak:
