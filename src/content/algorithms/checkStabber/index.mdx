## Checking stabber existence

A line *l* that intersects all segments of *S* is called a transversal or stabber for *S*.

This application shows the dual of a line segment in the xy-coordinate system. Drag a segment or one of its endpoints, or draw a new segment on empty space, and you will immediately see the resulting wedge in the right canvas. The corresponding wedge has the same color as the segment.

The black region shows the intersection of the wedges. If the intersection exists, there must be a stabber which intersects all line segments.
